MARSELO.SITE - UPDATE LOG
=========================
Newest update on top. Every update gets a new entry here.


UPDATE 4  (current)
-------------------
Laser cutter
  * Marselo no longer dies from the laser, with invincibility on OR off. He just gets
    cut and keeps living (his head keeps shrinking with every cut, forever).

Pick up the parts
  * The pieces on the floor (laser slices, loose bones after the acid) can now be grabbed
    with the mouse, carried, thrown, sliced again with the laser, or deleted with X.
  * Grabbing a limb the chainsaw ripped off no longer knocks Marselo over / makes him sad.

Weapons bar
  * Back to ONE row. The tools now shrink to fit the window instead of wrapping onto a
    second row (and scroll sideways only if the window is really tiny), so the controls
    text is visible again.

New maps  (left panel: map -> room / beach / city, remembered between visits)
  * Beach: sea, sailboat, sun, palm trees, umbrella, towel, sand floor.
  * City: skyline, sidewalk, road with lane markings and a crosswalk, street lamp,
    traffic light.

Cars
  * City map: about every 30 seconds (22-38s) a random car (sedan, van or taxi, random
    colour and speed) drives in from the right and runs Marselo over.
  * He gets flattened like a cartoon pancake (SPLAT!), stays flat for 3 seconds, then
    bounces back up (BOING!). Items and loose pieces in the road get bulldozed away.
  * "spawn car" button in the left panel: spam it to get many cars at once (works on
    every map, max 14 cars at a time).

Bug fixes
  * Fixed the map buttons being treated as gravity presets (would have set gravity to NaN).

New voicelines: being flattened (flat) and bouncing back (boing).


UPDATE 3
-------------------
Base: your index_1.html (your skins panel, walk AI and extinguisher were kept)

New tools
  + Acid syringe: thrust the needle into Marselo, hold F to inject. He melts from the
    top down (skin dissolves, skull and bones show underneath, acid drips and a green
    puddle) while he keeps standing. When he is fully dissolved, break.mp3 plays, he
    drops, every bone falls to the floor as its own loose piece, and he respawns after 3s.
  + Sleep syringe: same injection, but he sleeps for 60 seconds (eyes closed, lying
    down, floating "z"). He does not wake up and does not speak, no matter how much
    he is hit. He says good morning when he wakes.
  + Laser cutter: hold F, the beam slices whatever it crosses. Limbs and his head can
    be sliced; every slice becomes a real physics piece that falls, bounces and can be
    sliced again, over and over, forever.
      - invincible OFF: slicing his face kills him, he falls apart and respawns in 3s.
      - invincible ON:  you can keep cutting him into smaller and smaller pieces forever.

Syringes (how they work)
  * Needles now stick into him like the sword does (shared stab system), but they
    stay in; only a fast flick pulls them out.
  * He stops walking while something is stuck in him so the needle is not walked out.

Skins
  * The skins panel now shows a picture of every skin, also the locked ones (dimmed).
  * "Cool shades" are now black aviators.

Walking
  * Random walking is much slower (panic running when on fire is still fast).
  * Leg animation is slower to match.

Voicelines
  * New idle lines in combat mode (he now talks on his own every ~10-19 seconds).
  * New lines for laser, acid, sleeping and waking up.
  * More lines in the main talking mode (random, how are you, jokes).

Other
  * Toolbar wraps onto two rows now that there are 13 tools.
  * New file: break.mp3 must sit next to index.html.
  * New files: updates.txt (this log) and README.MD.
